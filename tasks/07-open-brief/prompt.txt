Invent a product and build its website.

It can be anything: a physical product, an app, a service, a place, a food, a tool. You choose the name, the idea, the story, the visuals and every word. The only requirement: make it memorable.

PAGE REQUIREMENTS
- A complete, convincing marketing website on one page: a hero, at least three further sections, and a footer. Real copy throughout; no lorem ipsum, no placeholder boxes.
- Fully responsive. It must look intentional at 1440x900 on desktop and at 390x844 on a phone.
- Every image, illustration or visual is made with HTML, CSS, inline SVG or canvas.

DELIVERY RULES
- Work autonomously. Nobody will answer questions, so make reasonable assumptions and keep going until the page is finished.
- Deliver a single file, ./index.html, with all HTML, CSS and JavaScript inline. No build step, no frameworks loaded from the network.
- The only network resource allowed is fonts from Google Fonts. No other CDNs, no external images, no APIs.
- The page must work when index.html is opened directly in a current version of Chrome, and when it is served over HTTP.
